Deleting your EasyNotes account

Draft for publication. Check the support email and deletion steps against the version of the app you publish.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This page explains how to delete an EasyNotes account and its data. EasyNotes has no usernames, email addresses or passwords. Your account is the twelve-word recovery phrase you wrote down. Notes are encrypted on your device before they reach a relay. A relay operator cannot read their contents or look you up by name or email, so deletion must be requested from a device that can prove control of the account.

Delete from the app

  1. Open EasyNotes, open the menu, and choose Sync & devices.
  2. Choose Delete account. If this device is not synced, choose Delete everything on this device.
  3. Read the confirmation, type delete, and confirm.

The device must be online and connected to the relay your notes use. If it cannot reach the relay, deletion does not complete and the app asks you to try again. Deleting the account also disconnects this device from that relay and forgets its address; the emptied collection stays on this device as the local one.

If you no longer have the app or device

Install EasyNotes on another device, choose the same relay in Sync & devices → Sync server, then use Use recovery phrase and enter your recovery phrase. Once your notes have arrived, delete the account from that device as described above.

Without the recovery phrase, nobody can prove the account is theirs, including EasyNotes support. Data on a relay that nobody can prove is yours cannot be deleted on request; it remains encrypted and unreadable.

What deletion removes

  • Lists and notes you own are requested to be purged from the relay, including lists you shared. The app waits for the relay to confirm deletion before it wipes this device.
  • Your encrypted account settings—including colours, labels, pins, reminders and device records—are requested to be purged from the relay.
  • Data on the device you delete from is wiped, including notes, keys and that device's identity.

What deletion does not remove

  • Copies other people already have. People you shared a list with keep copies on their devices. Those copies stop syncing, and the app tells them the list was deleted.
  • Lists other people shared with you. They remain with their owner. You leave each one as you delete your account: when its owner's device next syncs, your name drops off their People list and their device changes the list's key, so the deleted account cannot open it again. Your past edits in them remain part of those lists.
  • Your other devices' local copies. Each device is told the account was deleted. It keeps its copy of lists you own until you remove the account from that device too; lists you joined leave every device when the owner next syncs.
  • Relay backups. A relay operator may keep encrypted backups under its own retention policy. The documented EasyNotes Synology backup setup keeps backups for up to 30 days by default; another operator may use a different period. Backups contain encrypted data that the relay cannot read.
  • A deletion marker on the relay. A small opaque marker remains to stop an old device from re-creating a deleted list by syncing it again. It contains no note contents or account name.
  • Connection information already seen by a relay operator. A relay can see that a device connected, when it connected and its IP address. The operator controls its logs and retention. See the EasyNotes privacy policy and the relay operator's own privacy notice.

Questions

For help with the app or this deletion process, email easynotes@fraisl.eu. Do not send your recovery phrase, password or note contents. The relay operator is identified by its address in Relay settings; contact that operator about its logs and backup retention.
