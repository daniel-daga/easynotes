This notice explains what happens to information when you use the EasyNotes app or visit the EasyNotes website. EasyNotes is a local-first, end-to-end encrypted notes app. It does not ask you to create an account with a name, email address or password.

Who is responsible

For this website and messages sent to EasyNotes support, the data controller is [your full legal name or registered organisation], at [postal address and country]. Contact: easynotes@fraisl.eu.

The app does not automatically connect to a relay run by EasyNotes. You choose whether to use a relay and which one. The person or organisation operating that relay is responsible for its operation and its own privacy practices. Check the operator's privacy notice before using its relay.

Notes and app data

Notes, lists, labels, reminders, account settings and attachments are created and managed on your device. The recovery phrase is generated and displayed on your device. EasyNotes has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

If you use EasyNotes without a relay, the app does not send your notes to an EasyNotes server. If you choose a relay, the app encrypts note contents and attachments on your device before sending them. The relay stores and forwards encrypted data; it does not receive the keys needed to read note titles, text, labels, colours, reminders or attachment contents. Your recovery phrase is not sent to EasyNotes or to the relay. It derives information used to protect and find your encrypted account document; that encrypted document is synced like other data.

Encryption does not hide all metadata. The relay operator can see the IP address connecting to it, device public keys, opaque note identifiers, which devices connect to which identifiers, request times, data sizes and sharing requests. A relay or its hosting provider may process or retain connection and security logs, including IP addresses, according to that operator's configuration and policy. EasyNotes cannot set or verify another operator's retention period.

People you share a list with can read and copy information they receive. Removing access or deleting an account cannot erase copies already stored on their devices.

How long information is kept

Your local notes remain in the app's storage on your device until you delete them or clear that storage. The app does not send them to an EasyNotes server. A relay keeps encrypted data according to its operator's settings. Deleting an account asks the relay to remove the lists you own and your encrypted account document. Relay backups may retain encrypted copies for a period set by the operator. The documented EasyNotes Synology backup setup keeps backups for up to 30 days by default; other operators may use different periods. A small opaque deletion marker may remain on a relay to prevent deleted data from being re-created by an old device.

Support emails are kept for the period stated above. Website logs are kept for [period or criteria set with the hosting provider].

You can use the app without creating an identity account. You can use it without a relay, delete notes, and delete your account in the app. See the account deletion page for the steps and limits.

If the GDPR applies, these may include rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to data portability, subject to the conditions and exceptions in the law. You may also complain to [name and link of the competent data protection authority].

For information held by a relay, contact that relay's operator. EasyNotes cannot identify a person from a recovery phrase or retrieve note contents from encrypted relay data.

Security and changes

The app encrypts note content before it leaves your device, but no software, device or network can be guaranteed secure. Keep your device protected and store your recovery phrase somewhere safe. The EasyNotes app has not received an independent external security audit.
