# Deleting your EasyNotes account

> **Draft for publication.** The support email and deletion steps should be
> checked against the version of the app you publish.

**Last updated: 17 October 2026**

This page is for anyone who wants their EasyNotes account and data deleted,
including people who no longer have the app installed. It is the page to give
Google Play as the app's account-deletion link.

EasyNotes has no usernames, email addresses or passwords. Your account is the
twelve-word recovery phrase you wrote down, and every note is encrypted on your
device before it reaches a relay. Nobody who runs a relay can read your notes,
and nobody can look you up by name or email. That is why deletion is done from
the app, by a device that holds your account, rather than by a request to us.

## Delete from the app

1. Open EasyNotes, open the menu, and choose **Your devices**.
2. Choose **Delete account** (on a device without an account, **Delete
   everything on this device**).
3. Read what will happen, type `delete`, and confirm.

The device must be online and connected to the relay your notes sync through.
If it cannot reach the relay, nothing is deleted and the app tells you to try
again.

## If you no longer have the app or the device

Install EasyNotes on any device, choose the same relay in
**Relay settings**, then in **Your devices** choose **I already have twelve
words** and enter your recovery phrase. Once your notes have arrived, delete the
account from that device as above.

Without the recovery phrase nobody can prove the account is theirs, including
us. Notes on a relay that nobody can prove are yours cannot be deleted on
request; they stay encrypted and unreadable.

## What is deleted

- **Every list and note you made**, from the relay, including ones you shared.
  This happens the moment you confirm.
- **Your account's encrypted settings** — colours, labels, pins, reminders and
  the record of your devices — from the relay.
- **Everything on the device you delete from**: notes, keys and the device's
  identity.

## What is not

- **Copies other people already have.** People you shared a list with keep what
  is on their devices. It stops syncing, and they are told it was deleted.
- **Lists other people shared with you.** They belong to the person who made
  them. Your past edits in them stay part of those lists.
- **Your other devices.** Each keeps its copy until you delete the account on it
  too. Each is told the account was deleted.
- **Relay backups.** A relay operator may keep encrypted backups, under its
  own retention policy. The documented EasyNotes Synology backup setup keeps
  backups for up to 30 days by default; another operator may use a different
  period. Backups hold only what the relay held: encrypted notes it cannot
  read.
- **A deletion marker on the relay.** A small opaque marker remains so that an
  old device cannot restore a deleted list by syncing it again. It contains no
  note contents or account name.
- **What a relay's operator could already see:** that a device connected, when,
  and from which IP address. See the privacy notice under **About and privacy**
  in the app.

## Questions

For help with the app or this deletion process, email
[easynotes@fraisl.eu](mailto:easynotes@fraisl.eu). Do not send your recovery
phrase, password or note contents. Whoever operates your relay is named by its
address in **Relay settings**; contact that operator about its logs and backup
retention.

See the [EasyNotes privacy policy](/privacy) for information about the app,
relays and privacy requests.
