Updates since v0.1.3:

- Opening a note is smoother: the card turns into the note as it arrives, instead of the note appearing halfway through
- Nothing doubles up while it opens, and no blank frame flashes at the tap
- Closing a note shrinks back into its card, or fades away when the card has left the list
- The note is ready as it arrives for notes that open quickly, and one tap still opens one note when they open slowly
