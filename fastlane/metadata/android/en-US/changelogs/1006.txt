Updates since v0.1.5:

- Sharing is one tap: an invite link joins when it is tapped or pasted, even before the app is set up, and the guest opens the note straight away
- Everyone on a shared note sees the same colour, and a line typed on one phone appears on the other while you are still in it
- Select several notes at once and change them together: colour, labels, pin, archive or trash, with one Undo
