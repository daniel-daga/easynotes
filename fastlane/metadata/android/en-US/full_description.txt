<p>Notes and checklists in the style of Google Keep, where every note is encrypted on your device before it goes anywhere.</p>

<p><b>Local first.</b> EasyNotes starts on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose a relay in Relay settings. There is no built-in cloud and no default server.</p>

<p><b>Sync through a relay.</b> A relay stores and forwards encrypted notes and cannot read them: not titles, not text, not labels, colours or reminders. You can run one yourself; the relay is part of this project's source.</p>

<p><b>Share without an email account.</b> Send a link. The first person to open it joins the list, and edits appear live on every device. You can remove people again, and stop sharing altogether.</p>

<p><b>No account to sign up for.</b> Your account is twelve words you write down once. Enter them on another device to get your notes there. Without them nobody can restore your notes, including the relay's operator.</p>

<p>Also: labels, colours, pins, an archive, the trash, reminders, search that works offline, and notes shared into it from other apps.</p>

<p><b>Worth knowing before you start:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Notes kept only on this device are not backed up. Losing the device loses them.</li>
<li>Each relay keeps its own notes. Switching relay does not move them.</li>
<li>Deleting a list or your account removes it from the relay, but not the copies of people you shared it with.</li>
<li>The F-Droid build and the Google Play build are signed differently. You cannot switch between them without uninstalling, which removes notes kept only on this device.</li>
</ul>

<p>No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting. Free software under the GNU AGPL, version 3 or later.</p>
