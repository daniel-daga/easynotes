Updates since v0.1.6:

- An invite is now a link a messenger can tap: it opens the note, joins through your relay, and works before the app is set up
- The Share action shows the people on a note, and a rename now reaches every shared copy
- Crossed-off items stay out of a card's preview, so the card shows what is still to do
- Shared typing waits for a pause instead of arriving as small bursts
- Deleting your account leaves the lists you joined and removes you from their People lists
- A relay now always has its account, set up in one place under Sync & devices
