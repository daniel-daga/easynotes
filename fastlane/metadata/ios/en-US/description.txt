EasyNotes is notes and checklists in the style of Google Keep, where every note is encrypted on your device before it goes anywhere.

LOCAL FIRST
EasyNotes starts on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose a sync server in Sync settings. There is no built-in cloud and no default server.

SYNC THROUGH A SERVER YOU CHOOSE
A sync server stores and forwards encrypted notes and cannot read them — not titles, not text, not labels, colours or reminders. You can run one yourself; the server is part of this project's source.

SHARE WITHOUT AN EMAIL ACCOUNT
Send a link. The first person to open it joins the list, and edits appear live on every device. You can remove people again, and stop sharing altogether.

NO ACCOUNT TO SIGN UP FOR
Your account is twelve words you write down once. Enter them on another device to get your notes there. Without them nobody can restore your notes, including the server's operator.

Also: labels, colours, pins, an archive, the trash, reminders, offline search, voice notes, and notes shared into it from other apps.

WORTH KNOWING BEFORE YOU START
• Notes kept only on this device are not backed up. Losing the device loses them.
• Each sync server keeps its own notes. Switching servers does not move them.
• Deleting a list or your account removes it from the server, but not the copies held by people you shared it with.

No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting. Free software under the GNU Affero GPL, version 3 or later.
