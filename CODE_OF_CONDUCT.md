# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want EasyNotes to be a place where people can contribute without being
harassed, dismissed or made to feel unwelcome. That applies regardless of
experience, identity, background or how someone uses the app.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Review the change, not the person.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until there is evidence
  otherwise.
- Respect privacy. Do not post anyone's notes, recovery phrases, keys, personal
  data or private correspondence. A security report belongs in the private
  advisory flow described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. A declined change is not a judgement of
  the person who sent it.
- Keep discussions on the project. Do not use the issue tracker to campaign
  against a person or a group.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, intimidation, threats, slurs, sexualised attention, deliberate
outing, sustained disruption, and publishing someone's private information
without permission. Also not acceptable: using the project to build or promote
something that puts its users' safety at risk.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any project
space where someone is representing EasyNotes.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to **easynotes@fraisl.eu**. Include what happened,
where, and what you would like to happen. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept confidential.

The maintainer may warn, remove a comment, close a thread, temporarily block an
account or permanently ban it. A report about the maintainer can be sent to the
same address; if that is not workable, use GitHub's private reporting channel
for the repository. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is
itself a violation.
